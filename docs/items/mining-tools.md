# Drill, Hammer and Paxel

Three craftable netherite mining tools for digging a world about 2,000 blocks deep. All three have **2031** durability, like a vanilla netherite tool. The recipes will change, so check your recipe book.

## Crafting
Everyone knows the recipes from the moment they join. Each one uses the same shape: three blocks across the top and two netherite ingots as the handle.

| Tool | Top row (3 of) | Handle |
|---|---|---|
| Drill | Redstone Block | 2 Netherite Ingots |
| Hammer | Diamond Block | 2 Netherite Ingots |
| Paxel | Emerald Block | 2 Netherite Ingots |

The ingots go in the middle and bottom slots of the middle column.

## Drill
A netherite pickaxe with **Efficiency X**. That is far past the Efficiency V you can get from an enchanting table, and it breaks stone and deepslate almost instantly.

- It takes every pickaxe enchantment in an anvil, with the usual anvil rules.
- Efficiency stays at X. Adding an Efficiency book or using a grindstone does not lower it.

## Hammer
A netherite pickaxe that mines a **3x3** square. When you break a block, the 8 blocks around it break too, in a flat square against the face you hit. Mine a wall and you clear a 3x3 patch of the wall. Mine a floor and you clear a 3x3 patch of the floor. It always works this way, there is no sneak switch.

- Only blocks a pickaxe mines are broken, and only if a netherite pickaxe gets a drop from them. Everything else in the square stays.
- Bedrock and other unbreakable blocks are never broken.
- Blocks harder than the one you mined are left alone. Mining stone next to obsidian breaks the stone and leaves the obsidian.
- Every block drops loot and XP as if you had mined it yourself, so Fortune and Silk Touch work on all 9. Protected areas stay protected.
- Every block costs **1 durability**, so a full square wears the Hammer 9 times as fast. Unbreaking can save a use.

## Paxel
A pickaxe, axe and shovel in one. It is a netherite axe underneath, so it hits like an axe and takes axe enchantments.

- Mines pickaxe, axe and shovel blocks at netherite tier, at speed **9**, the same as the vanilla netherite tools.
- Right-click a log to strip it. Right-click copper to scrape off oxidation or remove wax.
- Right-click grass, dirt, podzol, coarse dirt, mycelium or rooted dirt to make a dirt path. There has to be air above the block, and you can't use the bottom face. A path costs 1 durability.

## Repairing and enchanting
- Repair in an anvil with **netherite ingots** (each one restores 25%), or combine two tools of the same kind.
- A tool never merges into a different item in an anvil.
- You can't use them as crafting ingredients, which also blocks repair in the crafting grid.
- Enchant them like their vanilla base: pickaxe enchantments for the Drill and Hammer, axe enchantments for the Paxel.
