# Elevators

Elevator blocks let you move between floors of a shaft without climbing. They are very handy in a world that is about 2,000 blocks deep: see [World layout](../world/layout.md) and [Depth](../world/depth.md).

## Getting one
Craft it in a crafting table: a ring of **8 white wool** around **1 ender pearl** (the pearl is in the centre). You get 1 elevator block. Ender pearls are hard to find underground, so staff may hand some out.

## Using it
1. Place elevator blocks directly above each other in the same column (same X and Z). You do not have to dig a tunnel between them.
2. Stand on an elevator block.
3. **Jump** to go up to the next elevator above you. **Sneak** (hold Shift) to go down to the next one below.

You arrive standing on top of the other elevator. A sound and particles play and a short message shows above your hotbar. If there is no elevator in that direction you see "No floor found".

## Limits
- Elevators are **50 blocks** apart at most by default. For a longer shaft, place an elevator at least every 50 blocks; you hop one at a time. Server staff may have changed this number.
- Only blocks placed from the elevator item count. Plain white wool does nothing.
- Make sure the space above the elevator you land on is free. The elevator does not check, so a block there can trap you.
- If an elevator is blown up by a creeper or removed some way other than mining it, the game can still remember it. You would then be teleported to empty air. Report it to staff.
- Pistons cannot move elevator blocks. Mining one gives the item back.
- Anyone can mine your elevator unless your base is protected.
