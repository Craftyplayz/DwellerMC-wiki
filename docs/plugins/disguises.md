# Disguises

You can turn into a block or a mob by wearing a renamed item on your head.

## How

1. Take any item that matches the lists below.
2. Rename it in an anvil to the exact words below (capitals do not matter).
3. Put it on your head (helmet slot).

| Rename to | Item | You become |
|---|---|---|
| `transform` | Any block (not heads or carved pumpkin) | That block. |
| `transform entity` | A matching item | A mob or entity. |

Examples for `transform entity`: porkchop is a pig, bone is a skeleton, rotten flesh a zombie, gunpowder a creeper, spider eye a spider, blaze rod a blaze, ghast tear a ghast, nether star a wither, echo shard a warden, emerald a villager, iron block an iron golem. Many boats, minecarts, projectiles and other items work too.

Take the item off to turn back. Dying, leaving the game or switching to spectator also removes it.

## Good to know

- Sneak twice quickly while disguised as a block to snap to the middle of your block.
- Your size changes with the disguise. Big mobs do not fit in cave tunnels.
- You are still a player. Hostile cave mobs, with their [levels](../mobs/levels.md), still go after you, and disguising does not give you a level or boss bar.
- Disguises are only visual. You do not get the mob's abilities.
- Disguise commands such as `/disguise` are for staff only.

See also [NPCs](npcs.md).
