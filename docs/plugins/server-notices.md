# Restarts and announcements

Staff can restart the server on a timer, post announcements, close the server for maintenance, freeze a player and hide themselves. This page covers what you see when they do. The numbers below are the defaults; server staff may have changed them.

## Quick facts

- Restarts are announced ahead of time. Wrap up or get somewhere safe when you see the warning.
- Announcements start with a bold gold **[Server]**.
- Maintenance kicks everyone except staff and keeps others out until it ends.
- Nothing here needs a command from you.

## Restarts

When staff start a restart countdown, you get a chat line and a title in the middle of the screen: "Server restarting in 5 minutes." The countdown itself is announced first, then again at 30 minutes, 10 minutes, 5 minutes, 1 minute, 30 seconds, 10 seconds and each of the last 5 seconds (only the times that are still ahead when it starts).

At zero everyone is kicked with "The server is restarting. Please rejoin in a minute." Rejoin after a minute or two. If staff cancel, a chat line says the restart was cancelled.

## Announcements

Staff messages appear in chat as **[Server]** followed by the text. They are not replies to you and cannot be answered.

## Maintenance

While maintenance is on, you cannot join and the screen says "The server is under maintenance. Please try again later." If you are online when it starts, you are kicked with the same message. Try again later; staff turn it off when they are done.

## Frozen

A frozen player sees "You have been frozen by an admin. You cannot move." They can look around but not move, and the message shows again if they rejoin while frozen. "You are no longer frozen." means you can move again.

## Staff you cannot see

Staff can hide themselves. A hidden staff member does not show in the world or the player list, and their join and leave messages are not shown.
