# Crafty Tools

Two special netherite tools: the **3x3 Pickaxe** and the **Paxel**.

!!! note "Not craftable yet"
    The tools exist, but they have no crafting recipe for now. Staff can hand them out.

## 3x3 Pickaxe
Breaking a block also breaks the other 8 blocks in the 3x3 square flat against the face you hit.

- Works every time, sneaking or not.
- Skips chests and other containers, liquids and unbreakable blocks (the bedrock roof and floor are safe).
- Drops are the same as a netherite pickaxe. Fortune and Silk Touch work on each block, and ores give XP.
- Costs 1 durability per block. It stops before breaking: it keeps 1 durability left, so mend it.
- Protected areas still apply to each block.
- Drops are picked up by [PickupBot](pickupbot.md) if you have it on.
- It can carry the [Veinmine](veinminer.md) enchantment.

Good for tunnels and cave walls, where most [ore](../world/ores.md) is.

## Paxel
A pickaxe, axe and shovel in one, at netherite speed. Mines one block at a time.

- Right-click with the axe side: strip logs, wood and bamboo blocks; scrape one stage of copper rust; remove wax.
- Right-click with the shovel side: make dirt paths; put out lit campfires.
- Each right-click action costs 1 durability.

## Commands
- `/craftylore`: refreshes the description on a Crafty tool in your hand.
