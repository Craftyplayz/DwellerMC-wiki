# Name colors

Change how your name looks in chat and above your head.

## Color your name
`/namecolor <color> [styles]`

- Colors: Minecraft names (`red`, `gold`, `aqua`, `darkblue`, ...) or hex like `#ff8800`.
- Styles you can add: `bold`, `underline`, `italics`, `strike`.
- Example: `/namecolor gold bold`
- `/color` lists the color names.

## Gradient
`/gradient <color1> <color2>` blends two hex colors across your name, for example `/gradient #8FF184 #F025FF`.

## Nicknames
Nicknames use the server's nickname command: `/nick <name>`. Ask staff if it is enabled for you. Nicknames are up to 16 letters long, not counting colors. Some words are not allowed, and you cannot copy another player's name if staff have turned that off.

## Notes
- Your colored name shows in chat and in the player list. Other plugins that format names may override it in some places, such as the tab list.
- Staff may limit some styles, such as the obfuscated "magic" style.
