# Tab list

Hold Tab to see everyone online. The list shows a header and footer with the server info, your ping next to each name, and names sorted by rank.

## What you see

- **Header and footer:** a welcome line, number of players online and staff online, your ping and some server info.
- **Names:** sorted by rank first, then alphabetically. Rank prefixes show in front of the name once ranks are set up.
- **Ping:** the number at the right side of each name.
- **Name above heads:** other players' names can show a rank prefix.

Staff in vanish do not appear in the list.

## Commands

Nothing needed. If staff turn on a sidebar or boss bar later, a toggle command is shown in chat.

## Cave mobs are separate

The `Lv N Type` name over hostile mobs, and the [elite boss bars](../mobs/elites.md) at the top of the screen, are not part of the tab list. They come from the [mob level system](../mobs/levels.md) and show up when a mob is within 12 blocks.

The depth line at the bottom of your screen is also separate.
