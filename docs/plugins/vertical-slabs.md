# Vertical slabs

Stand slabs and stairs on their side to build thin walls, pillars and shafts.

## Crafting
Put **one slab** or **one stairs block** alone in a crafting grid. You get the vertical slab or the sideways stairs of the same material. There are around 60 of each, covering wood, stone, bricks, deepslate, tuff, copper, blackstone, quartz and more.

## Placing
Click the **side** of a block. The slab stands up facing the way you clicked (north, south, east or west). It cannot be placed on the top or bottom face. Mining it gives the item back.

## Good to know
- Items show an enchant glint so you can tell them from normal slabs.
- Each one is made of a head block and a small display model. Huge walls built only from them can slow down weaker computers.
- Use `/defcorelib catalog` to browse what is available.
