# Protected areas

Some parts of the world, such as spawn, can be protected. Inside a protected area the usual rules change.

## What you may notice

- You cannot break or place blocks. You get a message that you are not permitted to do that here.
- You may not open chests or use doors and buttons.
- Mobs may not spawn there, so the area is calm. This also applies to spawn eggs and spawn commands. Mobs from outside can still walk in unless the area blocks them.
- Some areas show a greeting when you enter and a message when you leave.
- Some areas may be closed to you, or block ender pearls and teleporting out.

Protection covers the full depth of the world, so you cannot dig in from below either. If you are the owner or a member of an area, you can build there as normal.

## Commands

You have no WorldGuard commands unless the server gives you permission. Staff who set up areas use them. Ask a mod or admin if you want an area protected or changed.
