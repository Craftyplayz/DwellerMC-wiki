# Veinmine

Break one ore and the whole connected vein breaks with it. It only works with a tool that has the **Veinmine** enchantment.

## Quick facts
- Needs the **Veinmine** enchantment (max level 1) on your pickaxe, in your main hand. A plain pickaxe mines normally.
- Works on all ores: coal, copper, iron, gold, redstone, lapis, diamond, emerald, in stone and deepslate. See [Ores](../world/ores.md).
- Does not work in Creative.
- Breaks up to 100 blocks at once. Touching ores of different kinds count as one vein.
- Every extra block uses tool durability, like mining it by hand. Fortune and Silk Touch work.
- Your tool needs to be able to mine the ore (stone pickaxe on diamond does nothing).

## How to get the enchantment
- **Enchanting table:** it can show up as an offer when the level cost is 15 or more. It is rare.
- **Villagers:** it may be sold as a book if you find a librarian.
- **Anvil:** combine a Veinmine book with your pickaxe (costs about 7 levels).
- It is not expected in chests.

## Tips
- Cave walls hold most of the ore, so veinmining pays off in caves. See [World layout](../world/layout.md).
- Drops from a big vein can land far away. [PickupBot](pickupbot.md) only collects items within 5 blocks of you.
- Veinmine also works on a [3x3 Pickaxe](craftytools.md) that carries the enchantment.
- The server may require you to sneak while mining. If nothing happens, try sneaking.
- Protected areas stay protected: blocks you aren't allowed to break won't break.
