# Vaults

A vault is your own storage that follows you everywhere. It is safe from creepers, mobs and thieves, and you can open it from any depth: no need to carry loot back up from [deep caves](../world/depth.md).

## Using your vault
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/vault` | Open your vault |
| `/vault share <player>` | Let another player open your vault. They must be online. |
| `/vault unshare <player>` | Take their access away |
| `/vault shared` | See who can open your vault |
| `/vault <player>` | Open the vault of a player who shared with you |

Your vault has **2 pages** of 6 rows. Use the Next and Prev buttons to switch pages.

## Tips
- Only share with people you trust. They can take items out.
- Dying does not touch your vault.
- Put your spare tools, ores and [brews](breweryx.md) here before a deep trip.
