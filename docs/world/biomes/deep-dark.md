# Deep Dark

`minecraft:deep_dark` · Y **-1400 to -2000** · patches about 340 blocks across · about **2.4%** of the world

Minecraft's deep dark biome: sculk floors, sensors and shriekers that can summon the Warden. Rare and deadly.

## Blocks
| Where | Block |
|---|---|
| Walls | Unchanged (deepslate) |
| Floor (1 block deep) | Sculk |
| Ceiling | Unchanged |

## Decorations
Each sculk floor block tries these in order and stops at the first success:

| Decoration | On | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| Sculk vein | Floor | 5% |
| Sculk sensor | Floor | 0.4% |
| Sculk shrieker (can summon a Warden) | Floor | 0.15% |

Minecraft's own deep dark features also generate: sculk patches and sculk veins, plus geodes and dungeons. There are no water or lava lakes or springs. **Minecraft's ore generation is removed:** ores come only from the normal [ore system](../ores.md).

!!! danger "Wardens"
    The placed shriekers are the natural kind that can summon a Warden. Wardens are never leveled and never elite, but they are still Wardens.

## Mobs
**No mobs spawn naturally here**, the same as in normal Minecraft. Because nothing spawns, nothing gets [swapped for tougher mobs](../../mobs/spawn-mix.md) either. The danger comes from shriekers and from mobs that wander in from the caves around it.

## Ancient cities
Ancient cities only spawn in the Deep Dark, starting between Y -1450 and -1950. Deep Dark patches are rare, so ancient cities are too. See [Structures](../structures.md).
