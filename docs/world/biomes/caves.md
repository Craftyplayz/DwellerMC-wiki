# Caves

`dwellerbiomes:caves` · everywhere outside the other biomes' patches · about **86%** of the world

The default biome. Plain stone (down to Y -300) and deepslate (below Y -308), with Minecraft's usual underground features.

## Blocks & features
- No restyling: rock is plain [stone or deepslate](../layout.md#height-limits).
- Lava lakes, amethyst geodes and dungeons.
- Patches of dirt, gravel, granite, diorite, andesite and tuff.
- Sand, clay and gravel disks, water and lava springs, glow lichen.

## Mobs
| Mob | Weight | Group size |
|---|---|---|
| Spider | 100 | 4 |
| Skeleton | 100 | 4 |
| Creeper | 100 | 4 |
| Slime | 100 | 4 |
| Zombie | 95 | 4 |
| Enderman | 10 | 1–4 |
| Witch | 5 | 1 |
| Bat (passive) | 10 | 8 |

A higher weight means the mob is picked more often. These are the base spawns: deeper down, many are swapped for tougher mobs (see [Tougher spawns](../../mobs/spawn-mix.md)), and every hostile mob gets a [level](../../mobs/levels.md).

## Look & sound
- Fog color: very dark blue-grey (`#0e0e12`).
- Ambient cave sounds.
