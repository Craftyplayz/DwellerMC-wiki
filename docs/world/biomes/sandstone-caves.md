# Sandstone Caves

`dwellerbiomes:sandstone_caves` · Y **-20 to -700** · patches about 300 blocks across · about **2.6%** of the world

Desert-colored caves with sandstone and terracotta bands, sandy floors and husks. A custom DwellerMC biome.

## Blocks
| Where | Block |
|---|---|
| Walls | Repeating 3-block bands, top to bottom: sandstone, sandstone, smooth sandstone, sandstone, cut sandstone, sandstone, orange terracotta, sandstone, smooth sandstone, terracotta |
| Floor (1 block deep) | Sand |
| Ceiling | Smooth sandstone |

## Decorations
| Decoration | On | Chance per block |
|---|---|---|
| Dead bush | Floor | 3% |
| Pointed dripstone | Ceiling | 1.5% |

Also generates Minecraft's usual underground features: lava lakes, amethyst geodes, dungeons, stone patches, disks, springs and glow lichen.

## Mobs
| Mob | Weight | Group size |
|---|---|---|
| Spider | 100 | 4 |
| Creeper | 100 | 4 |
| Husk | 80 | 4 |
| Parched | 50 | 4 |
| Skeleton | 50 | 4 |
| Enderman | 10 | 1–4 |
| Witch | 5 | 1 |

No zombies or slimes: husks and parched replace them. All hostile mobs get a [level](../../mobs/levels.md) and can be [swapped for tougher mobs](../../mobs/spawn-mix.md).

## Look & sound
- Sandy fog (`#c9a76b`) with drifting white ash.
- Desert music.
