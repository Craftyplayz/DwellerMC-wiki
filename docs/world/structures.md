# Structures

Only four kinds of structure appear in the caves. Everything else in Minecraft is built on the surface, and here the only surface is the bedrock roof.

## Structures you can find
| Structure | Where (Y) | How common | Biomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ancient city** | Starts between Y -1600 and -400 | One attempt per 24 × 24 chunk area, at least 8 chunks apart | All cave biomes |
| **Trial chambers** | Starts between Y -800 and -100 | One attempt per 34 × 34 chunk area, at least 12 chunks apart | All cave biomes |
| **Mineshaft** | Minecraft's own height rules | 0.4% chance per chunk | All cave biomes |
| **Stronghold** | Minecraft's own height rules | 128 strongholds in rings around 0, 0 | All cave biomes |

"All cave biomes" means plain [Caves](biomes/caves.md), [Sandstone](biomes/sandstone-caves.md), [Lush](biomes/lush-caves.md), [Dripstone](biomes/dripstone-caves.md), [Sulfur](biomes/sulfur-caves.md) and [Deep Dark](biomes/deep-dark.md).

Ancient cities and trial chambers clear a room around themselves, so they aren't buried in solid rock. The start height is picked at random within the range shown.

!!! note "Ancient cities aren't tied to the Deep Dark"
    In normal Minecraft, ancient cities only spawn in the Deep Dark. Here they can spawn in any cave biome between Y -1600 and -400. The [Deep Dark biome](biomes/deep-dark.md) is a separate thing that only appears below Y -1400.

## The roof rule
Any structure that would reach **Y -16 or higher** is removed when it generates. This stops villages, temples, outposts and other surface structures from being built into or on top of the [bedrock roof](layout.md#bedrock-roof). It can also remove a stronghold or mineshaft that would reach that high.

## Dungeons
Dungeons (small rooms with a mob spawner) are a world feature, not a structure. They can appear in every [cave biome](biomes/index.md). Mobs from spawners are leveled like any other mob: see [Mob levels](../mobs/levels.md).
