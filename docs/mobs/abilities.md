# Mob abilities

Leveled mobs get extra abilities that grow with their [power](levels.md#power). These are on top of their stats and [gear](gear.md). [Elite mobs](elites.md) have further traits.

## Status-effect hits
Any leveled mob that hurts you, in melee or with a projectile, may give you a random bad effect:

- **Chance:** 35% × power (up to about 34% at level 50).
- **Effect:** one of Slowness, Weakness, Hunger, Poison or Mining Fatigue, picked at random.
- **Duration:** 3 to 6 seconds.
- **Strength:** level I, or **level II** from mob level 35.

## Archers
Skeletons (all kinds) and pillagers:

- **Aim:** their shots are steered toward you, more the higher their power. At full power they aim straight at your chest and allow for arrow drop.
- **Double shot:** a 50% × power chance to fire a second arrow 0.4 s later, aimed at you. Second arrows can't be picked up.

## Witches
From **level 25**, witches throw the strong (level II) version of their potions, where one exists.

## Slimes
Slimes and magma cubes split into extra pieces when they die: **+1 per 15 levels**.

| Level | Extra pieces |
|---|---|
| 1–14 | +0 |
| 15–29 | +1 |
| 30–44 | +2 |
| 45–50 | +3 |

## Spiders
Spiders and cave spiders from **level 10**: while chasing you and on the ground, 3 to 8 blocks away, they have a 25% chance every second to **leap** at you. Higher power makes the leap faster.

## Endermen
While angry at you, an enderman has a **30% × power** chance every second to **teleport next to you**: to a safe spot within 3 blocks of you, but not closer than 1.5.

## Creepers
Bigger blasts and shorter fuses. See [Mob levels](levels.md#mob-specific).

## Zombies
More likely to call reinforcements. See [Mob levels](levels.md#mob-specific).

## Silence
A weapon with the [Silence](../enchants/list.md#silence) enchantment can switch off every ability on this page for 5 s, plus a mob's elite traits.

!!! note
    Spider leaps and enderman teleports are only checked for mobs within 24 blocks of a player.
