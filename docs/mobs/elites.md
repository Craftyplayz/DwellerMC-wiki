# Elite mobs

Elites are rare, much more dangerous versions of leveled mobs. They have a gold name, a boss bar, flame particles and 1 to 3 special traits, and always drop a rare item.

## Elite chance
Any [leveled mob](levels.md) can become elite when it spawns:

- **1%** near the roof, rising to **3%** at the bottom. Use the [depth calculator](../world/depth.md#depth-calculator) for any Y.
- This includes spawn eggs, [spawn swaps](spawn-mix.md) and minions summoned by other elites. Mobs from mob spawners are never leveled, so they can't be elite.

## How to spot one
- **Gold name tag** with its traits: `Lv 38 Vampiric Blinking Zombie`.
- **Red boss bar** with its health, shown to players within **32 blocks**.
- **Soul-fire flame particles** around it while a player is within 32 blocks.

## Number of traits
| Level | Traits |
|---|---|
| 1–15 | 1 |
| 16–35 | 2 |
| 36–50 | 3 |

Traits are picked at random from the 12 below, with no repeats.

## Traits
Timed abilities run on their own timer, which starts at a random point for each elite.

| Trait | Name word | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Vampiric | Vampiric | Heals 30% of the damage it deals |
| Thorns | Thorny | When you hit it (melee or arrow), you take 20% of that damage back |
| Berserker | Berserk | Deals more damage the lower its health: ×1 at full health, up to ×2 near death |
| Shielded | Shielded | Completely blocks the first 3 hits it takes (shield sound) |
| Blink | Blinking | Every 8 s, if you're within 16 blocks, teleports 2 blocks behind you |
| Leaper | Leaping | Every 4 s, if 4 to 12 blocks away, leaps at you |
| Webber | Webbing | Every 10 s, puts a cobweb on your feet. It disappears after 5 s. |
| Burrower | Burrowing | Every 2 s, if it can't see you and is more than 2 blocks away, breaks the 2 blocks in front of it toward you |
| Summoner | Summoning | Every 15 s while it has a target, summons 2 more of its own kind (max 6 alive at once) |
| Frost aura | Frostbound | Slowness I to all players within 6 blocks, refreshed every second |
| Darkness pulse | Shrouded | Every 10 s, Darkness for 3 s to all players within 10 blocks |
| Explosive death | Explosive | Explodes when it dies (power 3, about a creeper). Hurts players but doesn't break blocks or start fires. |

!!! warning "Burrowing elites"
    Burrowers break any block up to obsidian hardness, including obsidian itself. They can't break bedrock, other unbreakable blocks, or blocks with contents such as chests, furnaces and signs. Walls won't keep them out.

!!! warning "Summoners"
    Summoned minions are leveled for the depth where they appear and can roll their own elite chance.

## Rewards
- **XP:** the normal [leveled XP bonus](rewards.md#bonus-xp), then **×5**.
- **One guaranteed [rare drop](rewards.md#rare-drops).**
- Elites don't get the extra loot rolls normal leveled mobs get.

Rewards only apply when a player kills the elite.
